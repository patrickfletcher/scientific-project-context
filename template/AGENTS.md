## Where Facts Live

Each durable fact belongs in one place, and every other place links to it.

- **This file**: what to honor before acting — environment, commands, protected inputs, conventions, routes.
- **`README.md` and other public docs**: what the project is for and how to run it, for readers including outsiders.
- **`.context/project.md` and the other documents under `.context/`**: where the science stands, what it rests on, and what is undecided.
- **`.context/index.md`**: the one list of those documents, generated by `spctx sync-index`. Keep no other list of them, here or anywhere else.

A fact that is both operational and scientifically material gets its one-line rule here and its reasoning in project context.

## When Guidance Conflicts

A conflict with the safeguards in `.context/scientific-practice.md` is not settled by rank: stop and ask. For anything else, use this order:

1. The user's current instructions.
2. This file.
3. Current project facts in `.context/project.md` and the other context documents.
4. Existing data, methods, code, tests, public docs, and established project conventions.
5. Your judgment, with important uncertainty visible.

## Working Style

1. Inspect this project before asking. Ask directly when a missing answer about scientific meaning, permission to change something, safety, or scope blocks safe work.
2. Take the smallest coherent step, and check it in proportion to what it could get wrong. Most work here is exploratory until something relies on it: move quickly, keep negative results, and give consequential choices a one-line reason.
3. Write in this project's vocabulary, including in your own summaries; tool and template terminology belongs to the tool. Lead with what was found and what it means, put logistics last, state each limit once, and leave out what did not happen, what stayed unchanged, and who approved it. Name things by what they are rather than by numbered codes.
4. Treat clean structure as ready for review, not as scientific, ethical, legal, release, or publication approval.
